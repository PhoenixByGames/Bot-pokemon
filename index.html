Restock date guidance

A retailer’s confirmed restock date is displayed separately from an estimate. The tracker cannot know a future delivery date unless the retailer announces it or provides a reliable feed. “Estimated” dates are signals to watch, not promises.

Data source: this project's scheduled checker and configured retailer product pages. Retailer websites can block automated requests or change their page structure. Respect retailer terms and never bypass queues, CAPTCHAs, logins or access controls.
